Departure enforcement

Any app can tell you it's time to leave. Outset does something about it.

Outset shields your distracting apps as your leave-by time closes in — and the only thing that opens them again is you, physically crossing your own doorstep.

One email when it ships. No account required.

How it works

01

It reads your route

Set where you're going. Outset uses Apple Maps traffic and works backward to the minute you need to walk out.

02

It shields your apps

As leave-by approaches, the apps you chose go dark. No timer to wait out. No QR tag to walk to. No code to tap.

03

You leave. It opens.

Crossing your geofence is the only unlock. Step past your doorstep and everything comes back on its own.

Privacy

No account. Outset runs no servers. Route and traffic come from Apple Maps; your history stays in your own iCloud, and we never see it.
